% against is of the hospital's closed cases, as in the analytics dashboard; it is shown only for hospitals with at least {{ min_closed }} closed cases. Against ÷ all cases divides the cases decided against the hospital by all its cases, open ones included, so a hospital with many cases still running ranks lower; it needs at least {{ min_closed }} cases. Open cases, and cases withdrawn, settled, returned or disposed without a stated result, are not counted as against. Averages are over closed cases; years need a filing date, so the KMC cases are not covered. % adjourned is a minimum: the record names the side that asked for time only some of the time. Most used lawyers are those on the hospital's side, with the number of its cases they appear in. The tracker holds only the cases the crawler has found.
