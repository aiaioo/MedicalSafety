How to read these lists

Patients' lawyers: who acts for the most patients, and who wins the most for them (cases won as a share of all their cases, or of their completed cases, those that have ended). The close rate is their closed cases as a share of all their cases for patients.

Volume: who has the most cases.

Wins: cases won, for either side, as a share of their completed cases.

Hospitals' lawyers: who acts for the most hospitals, and who wins the most for them (cases won as a share of all their cases, or of their completed cases). The close rate is their closed cases as a share of all their cases for hospitals.

Speed: the median time from filing to the end, or the median number of hearings, in the cases they won, smallest first. These count complaints only, not appeals or other applications, and leave out dismissals for non-appearance, at admission or at the first hearing. Caution: they still rest on very few won cases per lawyer, and how long a case takes depends on when it was filed and how contested it was, so speed may reflect the cases a lawyer happened to have, not their skill.

% closed is of the advocate's cases in the tracker. % won is of the cases the court decided (allowed or dismissed): a patient's lawyer wins when the complaint is allowed, a hospital's lawyer when it is dismissed (including for non-prosecution); settled, withdrawn, returned and open cases are not counted. % adjourned and % absent are shares of the hearings in their cases (where the hospital's side is known) in which the court's record says their side asked for time or was absent. The record names the side, not the lawyer, and names a side only some of the time, so these are minimums. Hearings per case is the median number of hearings held, separately for their closed and their open cases (open cases are still going, so that number keeps rising); the median gap is the days between one hearing day and the next across all their cases. Figures in grey rest on very few cases, hearings or gaps. The tracker holds only the cases the crawler has found, and the KMC records name no lawyers, so these are not a lawyer's full record.
